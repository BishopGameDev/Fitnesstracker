Progression rules

When you hit the top of the target rep range with clean form on every set, add the smallest available weight next time.
  • Leave roughly 2 good reps in reserve on most strength sets.
  • Do not increase running intensity and loaded-walking weight in the same week.
  • Use the minimum session on rough workdays rather than losing the third day.
  • Do not run in the weighted vest or backpack.
  • Skip the elevation mask; it is not part of this plan.

Minimum-session rule

A workout can still count on a bad day when you complete:

  • 10-minute warm-up
  • Three core exercises
  • Five-minute cooldown

Mark the workout complete and note “minimum session.” Consistency beats a perfect plan you stop using.

Recovery checks

  • Persistent joint pain: reduce impact or load.
  • Dizziness, unusual weakness, or racing heart: stop and recover.
  • Work performance dropping for multiple shifts: hold progression for one week.
  • Hydration, sodium, and protein matter more during early keto adaptation.
